# Measure_time - Chemistry Lab Operation

## Purpose
To precisely measure elapsed time for chemical reactions, processes, or kinetics studies. Essential for reproducible reaction conditions and rate measurements.

## How It Works
Stopwatches, timers, or data logging systems measure elapsed time. Automated systems use internal clocks. Accuracy ranges from seconds to milliseconds depending on the device.

## Setup
Timer/stopwatch (digital or analog). Or: automated system with timing capability.

## Usage
1. Start timer when reaction is initiated. 2. Monitor elapsed time. 3. Record time points for observations. 4. Stop timer at completion.

Results vary by specific application and conditions.
