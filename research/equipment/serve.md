# Serve - Chemistry Lab Operation

## Purpose
To present or transfer prepared chemical products in a controlled manner for analysis, use, or distribution. Typically involves aliquoting, labeling, and proper container selection.

## How It Works
Products are transferred from bulk containers to appropriate serving vessels (vials, bottles, test tubes) with proper labeling and closure. QC samples may be taken. Documentation of lot/batch numbers.

## Setup
Clean containers. Labels. Transfer tools (spatulas, pipettes, funnels). Balance (for solids). Documentation.

## Usage
1. Select appropriate container. 2. Label with contents, concentration, date, hazards. 3. Transfer product. 4. Seal container. 5. Document transfer.
