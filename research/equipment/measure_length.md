# Measure_length - Chemistry Lab Operation

## Purpose
To measure dimensions of solid objects, distances between points, or size of particles/materials in the lab. Used in materials characterization, apparatus setup, and sample preparation.

## How It Works
Instruments: rulers, calipers (vernier, digital), micrometers, or laser distance meters. For microscopic measurements: stage micrometers and calibrated eyepiece graticules.

## Setup
Measuring instrument appropriate for required precision. Calibration standard.

## Usage
1. Select appropriate instrument. 2. Zero/calibrate. 3. Measure object. 4. Read measurement. 5. Record.
