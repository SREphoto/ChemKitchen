# Potassium - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** K
- **Molecular Mass:** 39 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Solid
- **Melting Point:** 63.5°C
- **Boiling Point:** 759°C
- **Color:** Soft, silvery alkali metal.
- **Odor:** More reactive than sodium

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Extremely reactive
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
Soft, silvery alkali metal. More reactive than sodium. Reacts violently with water (ignites H2). Stored under oil.
