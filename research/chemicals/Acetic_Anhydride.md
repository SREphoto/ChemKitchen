# Acetic Anhydride - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** (CH3CO)2O
- **Molecular Mass:** 102 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Liquid
- **Melting Point:** -73.1°C
- **Boiling Point:** 139.8°C
- **Color:** Colorless liquid with sharp acetic odor.
- **Odor:** Reacts violently with water

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Corrosive, lachrymator
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.
