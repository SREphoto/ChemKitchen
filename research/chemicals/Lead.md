# Lead - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** Pb
- **Molecular Mass:** 207 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Solid
- **Melting Point:** 327.5°C
- **Boiling Point:** 1749°C
- **Color:** Dense, soft, bluish-gray metal.
- **Odor:** Malleable

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Toxic, cumulative poison
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
Dense, soft, bluish-gray metal. Malleable. Toxic to nervous system. Previously used in pipes and paint.
