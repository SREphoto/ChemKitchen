# Phenylacetone - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** C6H5CH2COCH3
- **Molecular Mass:** 134 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Liquid
- **Melting Point:** -15°C
- **Boiling Point:** 214°C
- **Color:** Colorless to pale yellow liquid with sweet odor.
- **Odor:** DEA List I precursor

## Safety and Hazards

- **Legality:** Regulated substance - check local regulations
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.
