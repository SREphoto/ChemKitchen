# Lithium Aluminum Hydride - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** LiAlH4
- **Molecular Mass:** 38 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Solid
- **Melting Point:** 125°C
- **Boiling Point:** 150°C
- **Color:** White to gray powder.
- **Odor:** Strong reducing agent

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Pyrophoric
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
White to gray powder. Strong reducing agent. Reacts violently with water. Store under inert atmosphere.
