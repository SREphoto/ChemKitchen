# Hydrochloric Acid - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** HCl (aq)
- **Molecular Mass:** 36.5 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Liquid
- **Melting Point:** -27.3°C
- **Boiling Point:** 110°C
- **Color:** Colorless to pale yellow liquid.
- **Odor:** Strong acid (pKa = -7)

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Corrosive, toxic fumes
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

Colorless to pale yellow liquid. Strong acid (pKa = -7). 37% concentrated solution. Stomach acid component.
