# Iodine - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** I2
- **Molecular Mass:** 254 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Solid
- **Melting Point:** 113.7°C
- **Boiling Point:** 184.3°C
- **Color:** Purple-black crystalline solid.
- **Odor:** Sublimes to purple vapor

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Corrosive, toxic
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
Purple-black crystalline solid. Sublimes to purple vapor. Essential for thyroid function. Used as antiseptic.
