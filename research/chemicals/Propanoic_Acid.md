# Propanoic Acid - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** C2H5COOH
- **Molecular Mass:** 74 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Liquid
- **Melting Point:** -20.5°C
- **Boiling Point:** 141°C
- **Color:** Colorless oily liquid with pungent, rancid odor.
- **Odor:** Weak acid
