# Methane - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** CH4
- **Molecular Mass:** 16 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Gas
- **Melting Point:** -182.5°C
- **Boiling Point:** -161.5°C
- **Color:** Colorless, odorless gas.
- **Odor:** Main component of natural gas

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Extremely flammable
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
Colorless, odorless gas. Main component of natural gas. Strong greenhouse gas. Simplest hydrocarbon.
