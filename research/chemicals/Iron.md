# Iron - Chemical Compound Research

## Basic Properties
- **Molecular Formula:** Fe
- **Molecular Mass:** 56 g/mol (rounded to nearest whole number)
- **State at Room Temperature:** Solid
- **Melting Point:** 1538°C
- **Boiling Point:** 2861°C
- **Color:** Silvery-gray transition metal.
- **Odor:** Magnetic

## Safety and Hazards
- **Primary Hazard:** Low toxicity
- **Safety Protocols:** Requires appropriate PPE including lab coat, safety glasses, and chemical-resistant gloves. Work in well-ventilated area. Consult SDS before handling.
- **HMIS/NFPA Rating:** Depends on concentration. Always check SDS.
- **Legality:** Generally legal with standard laboratory restrictions.
- **Insurance Needs:** Standard laboratory insurance required.

## Description
Silvery-gray transition metal. Magnetic. Rusts in presence of O2 and water (Fe2O3). Essential for hemoglobin.
